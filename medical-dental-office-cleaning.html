Questions

Before you book.

Do you clean clinical equipment?

No. Clinical equipment, instruments and sterilization stay with your staff and their infection-control procedures. We clean the room around them: counters, sinks, touch points, dispensers and floors.

Do you handle sharps or biohazard waste?

No. Those stay sealed for your licensed medical waste vendor. We remove general trash and recycling only.

Can you work around evening hours or Saturday clinics?

Yes. We start after your last patient leaves and build the schedule around your hours, including late nights and weekend clinics.

How does the free trial clean work?

After a free walkthrough, new recurring accounts get one trial clean at no charge. You see the checklist and the signed log before you commit.
