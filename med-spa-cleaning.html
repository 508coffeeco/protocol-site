Questions

Before you book.

When do you clean?

After your last appointment or before you open, whichever keeps rooms free for clients. Your times go into the written protocol so it never changes on you.

Do you handle sharps or medical waste?

No. Sharps and regulated medical waste stay with your licensed disposal vendor. We clean everything around them and never open those containers.

What products do you use around clients?

Disinfectants used according to the label, including the contact time, on every touch point, and low-scent products everywhere else. If you have products you prefer, we'll use yours.

How does the free trial clean work?

After a free walkthrough, new recurring accounts get one trial clean at no charge. You see the protocol in action before you sign anything.
