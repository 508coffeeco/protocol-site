A sample night. Your schedule is built around your close.

  1. 11:30 PM

    Doors lock. We walk in.

    Your closers head home. Our crew signs in and walks the room with tonight's checklist.

  2. 11:40 PM

    Restrooms first

    Partitions, locks and fixtures disinfected. Floors and drains scrubbed, not just mopped. Dispensers refilled for the morning.

  3. 12:30 AM

    The dining room

    Tables, chairs and the legs nobody looks at. Under the booths, high chairs, condiment stations and menus.

  4. 1:30 AM

    The bar

    Bar top and rail, the mats lifted and cleaned, floor drains cleared of buildup, cooler doors and glass polished.

  5. 2:30 AM

    Entry & host stand

    Front doors, windows and the host stand: the first thing your guests touch and the first thing an inspector sees.

  6. 3:00 AM

    Floors last

    Back of the room to the front door, so nothing gets tracked across a clean floor.

  7. 6:00 AM

    Ready to open

    The signed checklist is waiting at the host stand. Your opener walks into a room that's already done.

Questions

Before you book.

Do you clean kitchen hoods or grease traps?

No. Hoods and grease traps are specialty services with their own certifications. We focus on front of house, the bar, restrooms and entry. Back-of-house floors can be added at your walkthrough.

What time do you come in?

After your last closer leaves, and we're done before your opener arrives. The exact window goes into your written protocol.

How do you get in after hours?

During onboarding you give us a key or code and your alarm steps. Access details stay with the crew lead, and we lock up and arm the alarm on the way out.

How does the free trial clean work?

After a free walkthrough, new recurring accounts get one trial clean at no charge. You open the next morning to the result before you sign anything.
